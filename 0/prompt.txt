## P1 — D6 omits pre-discovery mechanism failures

`evaluateBinding()` calls `precheckCandidate()` before `discoverPaths()`. Invalid selector compilation or version-label validation can return `MECHANISM` even when no candidate-to-origin path exists.

Split D6 into:
- D6a: pre-discovery mechanism failures, independent of graph reachability;
- D6b: edge-evaluation mechanism failures, requiring a path that survives cycle preemption (`reach_A(s,t)`).

Update `complexity-proof.md`, `oq4`, and the equivalence matrix consistently. Do not claim that every mechanism outcome requires `reach_A(s,t)`.

## P1 — Canonical witness type conflicts with owner fallback

`api-migration.md` declares `CanonicalPathWitness`, but the owner decision permits choosing `any valid path` if D5 remains open.

Use a neutral `acceptedWitness` type/name until the owner chooses the contract. Explicitly distinguish:
1. pathKey-minimum witness;
2. deterministic but non-minimum witness;
3. arbitrary valid witness.

Do not expose a canonical guarantee unless D5 is closed.

## P1 — Rejection witness is underspecified

Define what evidence is returned for:
- `ERR_NO_TRAVERSAL_PATH`;
- `ERR_AUTHORITY_BINDING_MISMATCH`.

For no-path, decide whether the witness is a verifiable reachability/cut certificate or whether verification reruns reachability. For authority mismatch, define which precheck facts are included.

Separate independently verifiable evidence from diagnostics and from merely repeating the top-level code.
